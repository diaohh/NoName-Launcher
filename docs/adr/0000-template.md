# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Stand-by | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Commit**: `abcdef1` (where the decision landed, if it already did)

## Context

What forced a choice. The constraint, the bug that kept coming back, the thing that was
tried and did not hold. Write what a reader who was not there needs in order to judge the
decision — not the implementation.

## Decision

The rule, stated so it can be applied without reading the rest of the file.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes expensive, and what now has to be maintained by hand
because of it. Include the invariants a future change must not break.

## Alternatives considered

Each one with the reason it lost. An alternative with no stated cost is not an alternative —
it is a note.

Mark with **(reconstructed)** any option that was *not* actually weighed at the time and is
being written down now as a plausible road not taken. A record that cannot tell a real
rejection from a later reconstruction is worse than one that admits the difference.
